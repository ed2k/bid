2D opening

[ ] Benjamin 2D
    2D opening showing 8 playing tricks in any suit (Acol style), forcing for 1 round.

[ ] Flannery
    2D opening showing 11-15 HCP with exactly 4 spades and 5 hearts (4-5-x-x shape).

[ ] French 2D
    2D opening showing weak two in diamonds (6+ D, 6-10 HCP) or a strong forcing variant.

[ ] Multi
    Multi 2D opening showing a weak two in either major (6-card major, 6-10 HCP) or strong options.

[ ] Precision 2D
    2D opening showing 11-15 HCP with short diamonds (0-1 D) and 4-4-4-1 or 4-4-0-5 shape.

[ ] Strong natural 2D
    2D opening showing 22+ HCP or 8.5+ trick hand, natural and game-forcing.

[x] Weak natural 2D
    2D opening showing 6-10 HCP and a 6-card diamond suit.

[ ] Wilkosz
    2D opening showing 7-11 HCP with 5-5 distribution in two suits (at least one major).

    2H/2S opening showing 22+ HCP or 8.5+ trick hand, natural game force.

[x] Weak natural 2M
    2H/2S opening showing 6-10 HCP and a 6-card major suit.

[ ] Strong jump shifts 3

[ ] Weak Jump shifts 3
    Level 3 jump shift showing 0-8 HCP with a 6-7 card suit (preemptive).

    New Minor Forcing available for a player who passed initially.

[ ] Two Way New Minor Forcing
    2C is invitational checkback; 2D is game-forcing checkback after 1X-1Y-1NT.

[ ] TWNMF by passed hand
    Two Way New Minor Forcing available for a passed hand.
