## The candidate is a clean one-component swap, with a confound

`brill_distilled_relab.dsl` = shipped with ONLY `later_uncont` swapped:
open_uncont 252 and later_cont 829 rules are identical, later_uncont goes
752 -> 767. But relabelling cannot change tree structure (`id3_leaf_paths`
is purely structural, one rule per leaf), so 767 != 752 means the relab
slice was **refit under different settings** (`--folds 2`, so trained on
100% of rows; the shipped slice was fit with a holdout). Only 336 of 752
rule condition-sets are shared between the two. So the measured effect
bundles "relabel" with "refit" — a control fit (same command, no
`--relabel`) is needed to attribute it.

### Control fit settles the confound

`--folds 2`, no `--relabel` → **767 rules, same as the relab slice**, so
relabelling does not touch tree structure; 752 vs 767 is purely the refit
(train-on-everything vs the shipped 80% holdout). Control vs relab share
755/767 condition-sets; **69 leaves changed call**: 24 up a level, 7 down,
16 PASS→bid, 1 bid→PASS. So the objective is a small, systematic
contract-level *raise* — and 9% of the leaves costs 1.95 IMP/board.

## 6.84: the counterfactual (DD) target — 3x the change, none of the effect

`research/brill_dd_value.py`: one `CalcDDtable` per board (33,450 boards,
30 min on 3 workers, slower per board than the outcome solve: ~55 ms vs
~23 ms). `--relabel-dd` in brill_distill.py scores a candidate call by the
duplicate score of the contract it names — deterministic per deal, so no
winner's curse, and it can say "this game does not make". 13 tests.

Same shape as 6.82 (later_uncont only, same tree, same min-12/margin-0
guards, same control) so ONLY the label differs:

**204/767 leaves flip** (vs 69 for the outcome label); CV agreement with
Brill collapses 74.7% -> 58.4% (expected — it stopped imitating); mean bid
level 3.02 -> 3.22, game+ 33% -> 40% of bids.

| seed | net | se | t |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | −0.030 | .099 | −0.30 |
| 42 | −0.205 | .101 | −2.02 |
| 101 | +0.050 | .102 | +0.49 |

pooled **−0.062 ± 0.075, CI [−0.209, +0.086]**, 1 up 2 down.

So: safe and inert. 3x the leaf changes for 1/30th of the damage (the
winner's curse really was the outcome label's artefact), but zero gain.
Why: "stopped there" is one step of lookahead; a leaf's ~10 features do
not determine the hand, so the DD-best call varies within a leaf and the
argmax is only the average-best — and Brill's majority call is already
close to that; and IMP scoring is kinked.

**Three targets now tested:** imitation (baseline, one level light),
on-policy outcome (−1.89; empty once guarded), DD counterfactual
(−0.06 ± 0.08). The strongest computable objective is inert, so the
ceiling is not "wrong objective we could compute" — it is that a leaf's
feature view cannot determine the hand.
