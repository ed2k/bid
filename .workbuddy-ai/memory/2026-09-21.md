## The candidate is a clean one-component swap, with a confound

`brill_distilled_relab.dsl` = shipped with ONLY `later_uncont` swapped:
open_uncont 252 and later_cont 829 rules are identical, later_uncont goes
752 -> 767. But relabelling cannot change tree structure (`id3_leaf_paths`
is purely structural, one rule per leaf), so 767 != 752 means the relab
slice was **refit under different settings** (`--folds 2`, so trained on
100% of rows; the shipped slice was fit with a holdout). Only 336 of 752
rule condition-sets are shared between the two. So the measured effect
bundles "relabel" with "refit" — a control fit (same command, no
`--relabel`) is needed to attribute it.

## 6.84: the counterfactual (DD) target — 3x the change, none of the effect

`research/brill_dd_value.py`: one `CalcDDtable` per board (33,450 boards,
30 min on 3 workers, slower per board than the outcome solve: ~55 ms vs
~23 ms). `--relabel-dd` in brill_distill.py scores a candidate call by the
duplicate score of the contract it names — deterministic per deal, so no
winner's curse, and it can say "this game does not make". 13 tests.

Same shape as 6.82 (later_uncont only, same tree, same min-12/margin-0
guards, same control) so ONLY the label differs:

**204/767 leaves flip** (vs 69 for the outcome label); CV agreement with
Brill collapses 74.7% -> 58.4% (expected — it stopped imitating); mean bid
level 3.02 -> 3.22, game+ 33% -> 40% of bids.

| seed | net | se | t |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | −0.030 | .099 | −0.30 |
| 42 | −0.205 | .101 | −2.02 |
| 101 | +0.050 | .102 | +0.49 |

pooled **−0.062 ± 0.075, CI [−0.209, +0.086]**, 1 up 2 down.

So: safe and inert. 3x the leaf changes for 1/30th of the damage (the
winner's curse really was the outcome label's artefact), but zero gain.
Why: "stopped there" is one step of lookahead; a leaf's ~10 features do
not determine the hand, so the DD-best call varies within a leaf and the
argmax is only the average-best — and Brill's majority call is already
close to that; and IMP scoring is kinked.

**Three targets now tested:** imitation (baseline, one level light),
on-policy outcome (−1.89; empty once guarded), DD counterfactual
(−0.06 ± 0.08). The strongest computable objective is inert, so the
ceiling is not "wrong objective we could compute" — it is that a leaf's
feature view cannot determine the hand.

## 6.85: IMP units instead of points — also nothing

Last objection to 6.84: it optimised points, matches pay in IMPs, and the

## §6.86 — the ceiling diagnostic, and why its first version was worthless

Built `research/leaf_ceiling.py` to turn §6.85's "the representation is the
ceiling" argument into a number. Off the committed DD tables, per decision
in `later_uncont`: Brill's call +2.466, best call per leaf (out-of-fold)
+2.863, per-deal oracle +7.053 IMPs. Within-leaf agreement on the best
call **45.3%**, over 762 leaves wanting **7.7 distinct best calls** each.

**The measurement half is circular.** Added `--against LABEL=path` to score
a shipped .dsl in the same metric. It grades `relab` (outcome target —
independent of this metric) correctly: −0.910 predicted vs −1.887
measured. It grades `dd`/`ddimp` at **+0.667 / +0.712** where the match
says **−0.062 / −0.050**. `--relabel-dd` maximises this exact one-step
valuation, so scoring it with the same valuation can only agree with
itself. **Rule: an objective cannot be graded by its own training signal.**

Ground truth for the bias (`data/brill_outcomes.*`, 77,880 slice rows):
one-step value of Brill's call +198 pts, board actually paid +299, best
contract for that side +540; one-step == outcome on only 50.9% of rows.
It is *not* that the auction continues (94% of Brill's bids here do become
the final contract) — it is that a call made before any contract is
standing scores 0 while a call naming a making contract scores in full.

**Fix: `--emit`** writes a playable .dsl with the out-of-fold argmax
(fitted on fold-0 rows only, §6.84's ≥12-legal-rows guard). **111 of 767
calls change** — fewer than dd's 204, because a leaf often re-selects the
call it already had.

| seed | 7 | 42 | 101 | 202 | 303 | 404 | 505 | 606 |
| net | +.12 | +.13 | +.05 | −.03 | +.25 | +.06 | +.14 | −.06 |

**POOLED +0.083 ± 0.035, t +2.34, CI [+0.013, +0.152], 6 up / 2 down**
(12,000 boards). Between-seed sd 0.100 ≈ mean per-seed se 0.098, so no
seed heterogeneity beyond sampling noise. Pass-outs identical both sides
on all 8 seeds.

**§6.85's "objective is flat" is withdrawn in place.** The DD retarget
*can* pay; what was flat was §6.84's in-fold estimator. dd→ddoof
difference 0.145 ± 0.083 (t 1.75) — fold is the likely mechanism, not
proven on its own.

Metric also mis-ranks: `--candidates all` is worth nearly twice as much in

seeds).
