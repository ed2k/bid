## §6.88 — the retarget beats what actually ships: +0.082 ± 0.020

§6.82–6.87 were all measured against `brill_distilled_control`, which is
**not** what ships — it's a refit (--folds 2, 767 rules) built so the
relabelling had an identical twin. Calibrated that first:

- **the refit costs −0.120**: shipped vs control, seeds 7/42/101 → shipped
  nets +0.10, +0.05, +0.21.
- so §6.87's +0.212 vs control = **+0.093 vs shipped**. Measured directly
  over 6 seeds: ddimp_nox vs shipped **+0.093 ± 0.039**, 5 up / 1 down.
  Two routes, one number.

Better move: don't pay the refit and win it back — retarget the shipped
`later_uncont` slice directly.

    leaf_ceiling.py --dsl system/brill_distilled.dsl --candidates observed \
        --with-penalties --emit-no-doubles --emit-train all --emit-min 12 \
        --emit system/brill_distilled_shipdd.dsl

→ **148 of 752 leaves**, no doubles. One-component swap (all later_uncont,
rule ids identical, no X/XX).

Estimator validated, not new: the same command on the control tree
reproduces `ddimp_nox` with **134 identical changes, 0 conflicts**
(ddimp_nox also acts on 6 more leaves).

| seed | 7 | 42 | 101 | 202 | 303 | 404 |
| net vs shipped | +.09 | +.17 | +.07 | +.04 | +.08 | +.04 |

**POOLED +0.082 ± 0.020, t +4.17, CI [+0.043, +0.120], 6 up / 0 down**
(9,000 boards). Between-seed sd 0.048 well inside per-seed se 0.087.
Conservative per-seed reading +0.082 ± 0.035, t +2.30.

Statistically the same as ddimp_nox (+0.093) but 6/6 positive, half the
dispersion, no refit → keep `shipdd`.

**Harness check (do not skip this).** Every run put the control/shipped as
`--a` and the candidate as `--b`, so a harness favouring `b` would
manufacture all of it. `--swap` on seed 7: shipped −0.09 with shipdd as b;
shipdd +0.09 with shipped as b. Totals −133 / +133, won/lost 154/167 vs
167/154. Exactly mirrored.

~4.6% of the Brill gap (+1.780), from 148 leaf calls, obtained by deleting
twelve doubles and changing nothing else about the target.

**Not promoted to default** — not measured against Brill, central number
